# Brief

- Product: JotTrade, a private trading journal (this repository).
- Goal: a 45-second launch video for the landing page, Product Hunt, and social media.
- Audience: individual traders who want to learn from their own trades.
- Format: 1920×1080, 16:9, 30 fps, MP4. Silent-first: all meaning is in on-screen copy.
- Source of truth: the real app. No invented features, testimonials, pricing, or usage claims.
- Features shown (all present in `src/`): Exness MT4/MT5 CSV import with preview, manual trade entry (named in copy), dashboard metrics and equity curve, trading calendar, playbook check per trade, daily review notes, strategy plan adherence.
- Data: the fictional "Demo Trader" (`scripts/seed-demo.ts`) on the local dev database only.
- Audio: generated interface effects. No music, because no licensed track is available.
- Mode: autonomous (the user asked for a finished video, not a concept).
